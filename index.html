Rules and limitations

Basic FDP: Table 7.4 (p. 7-31). Planned extensions: Table 7.7 (p. 7-33), subject to §7.10.6. Normal operating duty finishes 15 minutes after final chocks-on (Table 7.9, p. 7-43). Minimum rest: §7.17 (pp. 7-57–58). Other scenarios—PIC discretion, split duty, standby, positioning, delayed reporting —require separate rule checks and are not automatically cleared by this preliminary calculator.
