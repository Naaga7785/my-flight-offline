MY FLIGHT - Archive consistency patch
- New archives save a snapshot of the pilot profile alongside every archived flight.
- Archived reports use the saved profile rather than the currently edited profile.
- Existing archived records without a profile snapshot remain compatible and retain their previously saved flight values and salary; their historical profile cannot be reconstructed.
- Tested with Node VM: archived salary, fixed block, profile, later route revision, and JSON backup roundtrip.
- No browser end-to-end testing was performed for this build.
